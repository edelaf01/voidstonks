Contact

VoidStonks is a solo project. If you found a bug, want to suggest a feature, spotted a wrong price, or have a question about how the data is calculated, the best way to reach me is through the Warframe forums:

  • Warframe Forums: w/Parcialsobriedad. Send a direct message or post on my profile.

When reporting a problem, it helps a lot to include: the tool you were using (Riven, Relic, Vosfor, etc.), the item involved, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots are welcome.

This is a hobby project, so replies may take a little time. Thanks for using VoidStonks and for helping make it better.
